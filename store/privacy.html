Cyberpunk HUD – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Cyberpunk HUD ("the app") is an app for Even Realities G2 glasses that shows a heads-up display with a map of your surroundings, the time, your speed and heading, and battery levels. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

Summary

  • The app has no server of its own and no user accounts.
  • We (the developer) do not collect, store, or receive any of your data.
  • Your location is processed on your phone and sent only to the two OpenStreetMap services listed below, solely to fetch map data and street names.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Permissions the app requests

Location

The app reads your phone's GPS position (latitude, longitude, accuracy, altitude, speed, and heading) while it is running. It is used to:

  • draw the minimap centred on you and rotate it to your heading,
  • show the name of the street you are on,
  • show your speed, heading, and altitude,
  • compute the distance and bearing to a destination you enter.

Location data is kept in memory on your phone only. It is not stored on disk (except a destination you deliberately save, see below) and is never sent to the developer.

Network

The app contacts only these third-party services, and only with the minimum data required:

  • Overpass API (overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems): receives a bounding box around your position (about 1.3 km square) to return OpenStreetMap roads, water, and parks for the minimap. Requested at most once per ~350 m you move.
  • Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org): receives your coordinates to return the street and neighbourhood name (at most once per 35 m / 12 s), and receives the text you type when searching for a destination.
  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com): receives your coordinates rounded to about 100 m to return the current temperature, at most once every 10 minutes or after moving about 2 km. Its privacy policy: open-meteo.com/en/terms.

The first two are operated by the OpenStreetMap community and are subject to their own privacy policies: OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy. Your IP address is visible to them as with any web request. The developer never sees these requests.

Data stored on your phone

The app saves your settings (units, map orientation, zoom, map refresh interval, demo-mode switch) and the destination you last entered (its name and coordinates) in the app's local storage on your phone so they are remembered between launches. You can clear the destination at any time with the "Clear" button or the glasses menu, and all of it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Glasses data

The app reads the glasses' battery level and connection state to display them. It does not use the microphone, camera, or photo album.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
